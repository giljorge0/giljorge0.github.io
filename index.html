Digital Brain

A provenance-aware neuro-symbolic memory system that reads your writing, finds the gaps, and thinks alongside you.

Started from a corpus of 500k words of philosophical writing with nowhere useful to live. The core thesis: retrieval is not enough — a personal knowledge system needs memory formation, maintenance, and gap detection, not just search.

The system ingests org-mode files, PDFs, LLM chat exports, Kindle clippings, and YouTube history into a graph of atomic ideas. A nightly consolidation loop deduplicates, detects contradictions, and decays stale nodes. A gap agent surfaces orphaned ideas, depth gaps (highly-referenced stubs), one-sided claims with no steel-man counterargument, and reading recommendations via Perplexity's live search. A query planner routes questions through symbolic graph traversal, semantic vector search, or temporal filtering depending on what is asked.

The engineering challenge that turned into a real learning moment: building the 30k-note "omni" graph crashed the machine. Diagnosis — 450,000 individual SQLite fsync calls plus an O(n²) cosine similarity matrix held in Python dicts (~450 MB) instead of NumPy arrays (~46 MB). Fix: batch transactions, numpy.argpartition for top-K without materializing the full above-threshold list, WAL journal mode.

Emergent Communication & Universal Language

What does a communication channel need to be for the receiver to know when a message is corrupted?

A two-part research programme. The theoretical essay proposes deriving a universal language from multilingual embedding spaces rather than linguistic intuition — treating the interlingua that emerges from multilingual seq2seq models as a window onto a language-independent world of ideas. The argument: languages currently partition the concept space suboptimally; a speaker-count-weighted centroid vocabulary would lower the entry barrier for every native language while making untranslatable concepts — saudade, schadenfreude, wabi-sabi — explicit rather than silently lost.

The experimental report (23 experiments) grounds this in referential games. The central empirical finding is a direct operationalisation of Deutsch (2011): discrete channels fail loudly — wrong-answer entropy rises monotonically with noise — while continuous channels fail silently, staying confident even when wrong. A zero-knowledge proof of communication (ZK metric) verifies that agents are genuinely using the channel without inspecting message content. Accuracy-weighted RSA confirms that symbol quality peaks at intermediate channel opacity, not at either extreme.

The universal language pipeline runs end-to-end: 4,863 concepts embedded across 12 languages → speaker-weighted centroids → translatability atlas (universal concepts at the geometric centre; culturally specific at the periphery) → compositional projection requiring only 3 antonym pairs to achieve 50% of max MRR gain → WALS-compliant phoneme inventory derived from cross-linguistic frequency, without explicit enforcement. Key result from the Shannon Pareto analysis: no protocol dominates on both compression and noise resilience. Message length is the dominant constraint in the full factorial grid search — more than temperature, bottleneck dimension, or production cost combined.

Neuropixels Behavioral Alignment & Neural Circuit Analysis

Building a biological clock from reward-responsive neurons when no hardware sync signal was recorded.

Eight Neuropixels 1.0 probe recordings from two head-fixed mice performing a joystick-pull task for water reward. The last AP channel was flat across all 89 binary files — no TTL pulses, no hardware sync. Phase 1 tested 440+ continuous features (solenoid artefact, MUA envelope, LFP band power, phase-amplitude coupling, reward modulation index). Best result: F1 = 0.12, RMSE = 144 ms.

Phase 2 used spike-sorted anchor units — reward-responsive neurons that fire reliably at a fixed latency after each reward event. Matching behavioral timestamps to anchor spikes via iterative σ-clipping and least-squares warp reduced RMSE to 1.6–18.7 ms across all sessions (10–100× improvement). A three-unit fusion for the hardest session reached 1.62 ms — near the practical floor given Kilosort's ~1 ms sorting jitter.

With alignment secured: 210 good units classified as Motor (21.9%), Reward (56.7%), Suppressed (17.1%), or Unresponsive (4.3%) by Z-scored PSTH thresholds. A population heatmap sorted by peak latency shows a clear temporal cascade from motor preparation through reward processing — consistent with cortico-striatal circuit organisation. PCA on the 201-unit PSTH matrix produces a 3D neural manifold where pre- and post-event trajectories occupy geometrically separated regions and the trajectory does not return to baseline within +1 s, indicating sustained post-reward population state change.

Music Genre Classification via Dimensionality Reduction & Causal Analysis

When does a feature selection method pick the right features for the wrong reasons?

Eight mutual information feature selection methods applied to a 10-class music genre dataset (35k tracks): MIM, MIFS, mRMR, CMIM, JMI, DISR, maxMIFS, DMIM. Two classifiers evaluated across PCA, Kernel PCA (RBF/Polynomial/Laplacian via Nyström), and all FS subsets. CMIM retains 93.9% of the full-model Macro F1 with only 6 features; MIFS is worst because its unscaled redundancy penalty eventually selects maximally independent but genre-uninformative features.

Causal extension motivated by Suter et al. (ICML 2019): PC algorithm recovers the causal skeleton among features; do-calculus interventional curves separate causal from confounded associations. Key result — popularity does not causally determine genre. The observational correlation exists because Classical music has low streaming popularity, but intervening on popularity leaves genre prediction unchanged. Hip-Hop and Rap are acoustically indistinguishable in this dataset across all 11 audio features; the classifier is correctly reflecting the limits of available information.

Post-Quantum Mersenne Cryptosystem

A public-key cryptosystem whose security reduces to the hardness of distinguishing a low-Hamming-weight combination from a uniform random string.

Full implementation of Aggarwal-Joux-Prakash-Santha (CRYPTO 2018). Security rests on the Mersenne Low Hamming Combination (MLHC) assumption — analogous to LWE but over Mersenne prime fields. Built incrementally: bit-by-bit encryption → repetition code → Reed-Muller RM(m,1) → KEM with Fujisaki-Okamoto IND-CCA2 transform via SHAKE-256 / SHA-256 random oracles.

Demonstrated the weak key attack — if F, G < √p, continued fractions recover the private key in polynomial time. Security analysis: LLL-based attack (quantum cost O(2^h), motivating h = λ directly), meet-in-the-middle, brute force, all tabulated in log-space for n = 756,839. Empirical pseudorandomness test: Shapiro-Wilk and KS both marginally reject normality at n = 127 but Q-Q R² = 0.995 — a finite-sample artefact that would vanish at n = 756,839.

Reed-Muller vs. Repetition code comparison: RM gives a deterministic correction guarantee and formal proof of minimum distance at the cost of a 127 MB generator matrix and O(n·m) decoding. Repetition gives practical deployment via majority vote (0 KB storage, O(n) decode) and δ < 2⁻²³⁹ at λ = 256.

MacroPCA — Robust PCA with Cellwise & Rowwise Outliers

Subspace angle is not an absolute quality metric.

Replication and extension of Hubert, Rousseeuw & Van den Bossche (Technometrics 2019). Three methods compared: ICPCA (handles missing values), MROBPCA (handles missing + rowwise), MacroPCA (handles all three simultaneously). MROBPCA fails structurally when d > n — MCD requires h > d, and with d = 200, n = 100 the covariance matrix is singular. Documented as an empirical result rather than an implementation bug.

Extension to the 2022 World Bank Development Indicators (253 country aggregates × 45 indicators). MacroPCA separates size-driven anomalies (GDP aggregates) from governance anomalies (Afghanistan, Zimbabwe, Venezuela) — a meaningful domain decomposition invisible to ICPCA. On the contaminated World Bank matrix, the subspace angle ranking reverses: ICPCA scores better, but this is a measurement artefact — the reference subspace is itself aggregate-influenced. Detection metrics (rowwise sensitivity 1.00) unambiguously favor MacroPCA.

RNA Binding Affinity — CNN + BiLSTM + Attention

Modeling RBFOX1-RNA binding: local motifs via CNN, global context via BiLSTM, interpretability via attention.

RBFOX1 recognises a short 5–6 nucleotide RNA motif, but the full strand influences binding affinity through structural and chemical interactions. CNN captures local binding regions (three convolutional layers, kernels 5/7/9). Bidirectional LSTM captures global 5'→3' and 3'→5' context. Grid search over learning rate, dropout, and hidden width; best models: CNN Spearman r = 0.665, BiLSTM r = 0.681.

Attention layer added to BiLSTM. Attention heatmaps on test sequences show focused attention on the GCA nucleotides in high-affinity sequences; low-affinity sequences produce diffuse, uniform attention — consistent with the biological role of the (U)GCAUG motif. The second attention head mirrors the first exactly, confirming that a single head is sufficient for this localized binding site. Final model Spearman r = 0.679, MSE = 0.314, no significant overfitting.

Jean Baudrillard

Simulation has consumed the real so completely that the crime — reality's disappearance — is perfect: no body, no evidence, no criminal. Baudrillard's most concentrated argument that the world has been replaced by its own image, and that this substitution happened without anyone noticing. Information and Entropy — Lecture Notes

Seth Lloyd · MIT OpenCourseWare

Entropy as the measure of what we do not know rather than what we cannot access. Lloyd builds thermodynamics from information theory rather than the reverse, connecting Maxwell's demon to Landauer's principle to quantum computation. The cleanest treatment of why erasing a bit costs energy.

The authoritative modern treatment, connecting classical Shannon theory to contemporary statistics and machine learning.
